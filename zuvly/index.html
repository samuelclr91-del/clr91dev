The project

Less switching. More perspective.

Zuvly brings app reports, reviews and subscription events into a focused interface for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad. The project is still in development; the screen shown here uses sample data.

01

See the wider picture

Explore downloads and revenue reports by period, with a visual overview of your apps.

02

Listen to your customers

Read imported customer reviews and explore the feedback behind the numbers.

03

Follow subscription events

Explore the subscription events received by a configured workspace, alongside the rest of your app activity.
