Trackle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short: Trackle works without an account and keeps your data on your device. If you sign in or turn on sync, a copy of your data is stored in the cloud so it can follow you across devices. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you.

1. Your data stays on your device by default

Everything you enter in Trackle — subscriptions, transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, profiles and your profile photo — is stored on your device. You can use the free plan without creating an account.

If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy is stored in your own private iCloud storage, managed by Apple. We cannot access it.

2. Account and cloud copy (optional)

If you sign in with Apple or Google, Trackle creates an account using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). We receive an account identifier and the name and email address that Apple or Google share with the app (Apple lets you hide your email).

While you are signed in, Trackle uploads a copy of your data — subscriptions, profiles, monthly history, settings, accounts, categories, budgets and transactions — linked to your account identifier, so it stays in sync across your devices and can be restored. It travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and is protected at rest by Google, but it is not end-to-end encrypted. We only access it to operate the service and to give you support.

Using an account is optional: Trackle works without one.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple and the App Store. Trackle never sees your card or billing details. To unlock Pro features and restore purchases, Trackle uses RevenueCat, which receives an app user identifier, your purchase history and, if you have signed in, your email address, your name, the sign-in method and basic app information (such as the app version). See Apple's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. Apple Intelligence

Trackle uses Apple Intelligence to power its financial-analysis assistant. This processing runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device foundation model. No data is sent to external servers for this purpose.

On devices that don't support Apple Intelligence, the assistant falls back to a local rules-based engine — also without any server connection.

5. Other services Trackle connects to

To show icons and check for updates, Trackle contacts the services below. Each receives only what its request needs and, as with any internet connection, your IP address:

  • Apple (iTunes Search): the name of the subscription you type, to find its app icon.
  • DuckDuckGo and Google (favicons): the website domain of a service you add, to fetch its icon.
  • Apple (App Store): Trackle's app identifier, to check whether a new version exists, and a request to apple.com to check your connection.
  • Apple and Google sign-in: only if you choose to sign in.

These services do not receive your amounts, categories, transactions or notes.

6. What is handled only on your device

  • Face ID / Touch ID: handled by iOS. Trackle never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Importing a screenshot: Trackle reads the screenshot you choose (or your latest one, if you allow access to Photos) and analyses it on the device with Apple's text recognition. The image is not uploaded.
  • Spotlight, Siri shortcuts and widgets: they use data kept on your device.

7. What we don't do

Trackle has no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, contains no analytics or crash-reporting libraries, and we never sell your data or share it for marketing. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated reports that cannot identify you.

8. Where data is processed

Google (Firebase) and RevenueCat may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. These providers state that they use safeguards recognised by EU law, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. How long we keep it and how to delete it

Data stored only on your device disappears when you delete it in the app or uninstall Trackle.

In the app, go to Settings › Profile › Delete my account: it immediately deletes your account and your backup in Firebase, and removes your data from your device and from iCloud. "Delete all data" does the same without removing the account. The purchase record kept by RevenueCat (identifier, email, name) is deleted on request at the email address below, within 30 days.

10. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Contact us at the address below. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es). The controller is Samuel Rodríguez Díez (Clr91dev).

11. Security

Cloud copies travel over HTTPS and are protected by Google's infrastructure. On your device, your data is protected by the security features of iOS (passcode, Face ID, disk encryption), and you can lock Trackle with Face ID or Touch ID.

12. Children's data

Trackle is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect data from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information, please contact us so we can take the necessary action.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy. Any changes will be published on this page with the corresponding update date. We recommend reviewing it from time to time.
